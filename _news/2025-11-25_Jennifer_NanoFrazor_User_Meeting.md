[Jennifer Dong](https://marlandlab.github.io/profiles/) attended the NanoFrazor UK user meeting in Liverpool, where she presented her work on silicon nanowire fabrication using [thermal scanning probe lithography](https://heidelberg-instruments.com/product/nanofrazor/). The meeting also provided an opportunity to discuss optimisation of NanoFrazor patterning parameters with R&D engineers from Heidelberg Instruments Nano.
