We are delighted to have been awarded a [Royal Society Research Grant](https://royalsociety.org/grants/research-grants/) to develop highly sensitive silicon nanowire sensors for detecting neurotransmitter release. This project will bring together nanoelectronics and neuroscience to develop new bioelectronic tools.
