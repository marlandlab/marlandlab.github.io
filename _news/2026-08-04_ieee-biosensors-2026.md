[Jamie Marland](/profiles/) attended [IEEE BioSensors 2026](https://ieee-biosensors.org/2026) in Bangkok, Thailand, and presented a poster featuring [Jennifer Dong's](/profiles/) PhD research: “Aptamer Functionalised Silicon Nanowire Field-Effect Transistors for Selective Dopamine Detection”. The conference was a great opportunity to hear how the field is moving beyond ever-lower detection limits towards continuous, multiplexed biosensors designed with real-world translation in mind.
