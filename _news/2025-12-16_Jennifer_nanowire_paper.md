Our paper on [top-down fabrication of silicon nanowire field-effect transistors using thermal scanning probe lithography](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsanm.5c05208) has been published in ACS Applied Nano Materials. Congratulations to [Jennifer Dong](https://marlandlab.github.io/profiles/) and everyone involved in demonstrating this flexible approach to fabricating SiNW FETs for sensing applications.
