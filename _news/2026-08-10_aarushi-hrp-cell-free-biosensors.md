Our new paper, [“Horseradish peroxidase as an electrochemical reporter protein for cell-free biosensors”](https://doi.org/10.1093/synbio/ysag009), has been published in *Synthetic Biology*. The study was led by PhD student [Aarushi Ruhela](/profiles/), working with our collaborator [Dr Nadanai Laohakunakorn](https://nadanai263.github.io/), and shows that cell-free-expressed horseradish peroxidase can provide an electrochemical output as an alternative to optical reporters for future portable biosensors.
