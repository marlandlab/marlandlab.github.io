[Jamie Marland](/profiles/) attended [IEEE BioSensors 2026](https://ieee-biosensors.org/2026) in Bangkok, Thailand, and presented a poster on “Aptamer Functionalised Silicon Nanowire Field-Effect Transistors for Selective Dopamine Detection”. It was a great opportunity to hear how the field is moving beyond detection limits towards continuous, multiplexed biosensors designed with for real world translation.
